How it works: curated crypto questions appear in the app. This app verifies your signed YES/NO forecast and places its proof in Technocore’s public flop-predictions room. FLOP supports Substrate wallets, but this DID signing flow requires an Ed25519 did:key. A DID signature proves control of a key, not a real-world identity. Technocore protocol · FLOP network status.
